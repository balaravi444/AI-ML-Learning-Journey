📖 ABCDE Self-Check Guide

Watch for these warning signs in moles:

A
Asymmetry
Two halves don't match
B
Border
Irregular, ragged edges
C
Color
Multiple shades, uneven
D
Diameter
Larger than 6mm (pencil eraser)
E
Evolution
Any change in size, shape, color, or a new symptom (bleeding, itching)

If you notice any ABCDE signs → see a dermatologist immediately.
